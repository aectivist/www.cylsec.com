About Us

What is CYLVERN SECURITY?

CylSec is a student-focused cybersecurity organisation that provides structured, hands-on learning experiences to help students develop foundational skills in cybersecurity with real-world application.

We promote practical education through workshops, training sessions, and community-based activities that support continuous skill development. Our environment is designed to be accessible — beginners can learn safely, apply concepts in real scenarios, and gradually build confidence.
