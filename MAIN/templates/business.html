What We Offer

Services for Organisations

Every engagement begins with a conversation. We tailor scope and deliverables to your exact needs.

Penetration Testing

We simulate real-world attacks against your systems, applications, and networks to identify vulnerabilities before malicious actors do. Every test is conducted ethically and under a clearly defined scope agreed upon in advance.

  • Web application penetration testing (OWASP methodology)
  • Network infrastructure and internal/external perimeter testing
  • Social engineering & phishing simulation assessments
  • Detailed written reports with executive summaries and remediation guidance
  • Post-test debrief and re-test upon remediation

Security Consulting

Not sure where your biggest risks are? Our consulting team provides strategic security reviews and actionable recommendations to improve your security posture without the complexity of a full engagement.

  • Security posture review and gap analysis
  • Policy and procedure development for security teams
  • Compliance gap assessment (e.g. ISO 27001, NIST, CIS Controls)
  • Third-party vendor risk review
  • Incident response planning and tabletop exercises

Security Awareness Training

Human error remains the leading cause of breaches. We deliver targeted training programmes that equip your staff with the awareness and habits to recognise and respond to threats in their daily work.

  • Interactive workshops for all staff levels
  • Phishing simulation campaigns with detailed reporting
  • Custom content built around your industry and threat landscape
  • Measurable outcomes and repeat assessments to track improvement
