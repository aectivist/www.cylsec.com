Overview

Our Commitment

Cylvern Security (CYLSEC) operates online platforms including the CTF platform at cylsec.com, our community Discord, and this marketing site. We recognise that security researchers play an important role in identifying vulnerabilities that our own team may have missed.

This Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP) describes what systems are in scope, what types of testing are permitted, how to submit a report, and what you can expect from us in return. It is not a bug bounty programme — we cannot offer financial rewards at this time — but we do promise to take every valid report seriously and to recognise researchers who help make CYLSEC safer.

Scope

What's In Scope

Only the assets listed below are authorised for testing under this policy. Testing any asset not listed here is out of scope and may violate applicable law.

cylsec.com

The main marketing and information site. Web application vulnerabilities only — no infrastructure attacks.

IN SCOPE

CTF Platform

The CTF application at ctf.cylsec.com (auth, challenge submission, user accounts).

Note: Intentionally vulnerable CTF challenge instances are excluded — those are meant to be broken.

IN SCOPE

Discord Server

Our community Discord. Report phishing, impersonation, or bot abuse to us directly via Discord DM to an admin — do not test automated systems.

REPORT ONLY

Out of Scope

  • ✗ CTF challenge instances — these are intentionally vulnerable; exploiting them is the challenge
  • ✗ Third-party services we use (hosting providers, email services, CDNs, etc.)
  • ✗ Physical infrastructure, office networks, or personal devices of CYLSEC members
  • ✗ Social engineering or phishing attacks against CYLSEC staff or members
  • ✗ Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks of any kind
  • ✗ Automated scanning that impacts platform availability for other users
  • ✗ Accessing, modifying, or deleting data belonging to other users
Rules of Engagement

Responsible Testing Guidelines

To qualify for safe harbour under this policy you must adhere to all of the following:

Use Your Own Account

Only test against accounts you own or have explicit written permission to test. Never access another user's data.

Minimal Footprint

Use the minimum access needed to demonstrate the vulnerability. Stop immediately once impact is proven.

Keep It Confidential

Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until we've confirmed it is resolved, or 90 days have passed since your report — whichever comes first.

No Data Exfiltration

Do not exfiltrate, store, share, or retain any data you access during testing beyond what's needed to write the report.

No Availability Impact

Do not perform any test that degrades performance or availability of CYLSEC services for other users.

Report Clearly

Provide enough detail for us to reproduce the issue: steps to reproduce, impact, affected URL, and any PoC code or screenshots.

Priority

Vulnerability Categories

The table below describes the types of findings we are most interested in, grouped by impact level. We review all valid submissions regardless of severity.

Severity Examples Response SLA
Critical Remote code execution, authentication bypass, full account takeover, SQL injection with data access, privilege escalation to admin Acknowledged within 2 days, patch within 7 days
High Stored XSS, IDOR exposing other users' private data, broken access control, CSRF on sensitive actions, insecure direct object references Acknowledged within 3 days, patch within 14 days
Medium Reflected XSS, open redirect, session fixation, verbose error messages with sensitive info, missing security headers with demonstrable impact Acknowledged within 5 days, patch within 30 days
Low / Info Missing security headers (no impact), software version disclosure, rate-limiting issues, clickjacking without sensitive actions Acknowledged within 5 days, addressed at discretion

Legal

Safe Harbour

CYLSEC will not pursue legal action against security researchers who discover and report vulnerabilities in good faith, provided they comply with all conditions of this policy.

"Good faith" means: you did not access data beyond what was necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability, you did not degrade service availability, you did not use the vulnerability for personal gain, and you reported it to us promptly rather than disclosing it publicly or to a third party first.

This safe harbour applies only to the in-scope assets listed in this policy and only for activities that comply with these guidelines. It does not authorise unlawful access to systems belonging to third parties.

Submit

Report a Vulnerability

Send your report directly to security@cylsec.com with the subject line [VDP] <brief description>. Include the information below for the fastest resolution.

What to Include in Your Report

  • Affected URL, endpoint, or feature
  • Step-by-step reproduction instructions
  • Your assessment of severity and potential impact
  • Screenshots, HTTP request/response, or PoC code (as applicable)
  • Your preferred name or handle for Hall of Fame credit (optional)
  • Whether you'd like us to coordinate a public disclosure date

Encrypt Your Report (Optional)

For sensitive findings, you may encrypt your email using our PGP key. Contact us at security@cylsec.com to request our public key, or look it up on keys.openpgp.org by searching for security@cylsec.com.
