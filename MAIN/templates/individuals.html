How to Join CYLSEC

Free to join. No experience required.

1

Create an Account

Sign up on the CTF platform at cylsec.com. All you need is an email address — it takes under a minute.

2

Join the Discord

Connect with the community, ask questions, find study partners, and stay updated on workshops and events.

3

Pick a Pathway

Choose CTF challenges, follow Academy learning paths, or apply for Private Mentorship based on your goals.

4

Start Hacking

Earn XP, solve challenges, complete labs, and climb the leaderboard. Your rank reflects your real-world skills.

CTF Platform

Prove Your Skills in the Arena

CYLSEC's CTF (Capture The Flag) platform is a live, competitive hacking environment where you solve security challenges modelled on real vulnerabilities. Each solved challenge awards XP and moves you up the global leaderboard.

  • Web, Pwn, Forensics, Reverse Engineering, Crypto & OSINT categories
  • Docker-isolated instances — your own private lab environment per challenge
  • XP-based rank system from E-Rank Novice to S+ Mythic
  • Hint system and community write-ups after solving
  • Competitive leaderboard updated in real time
  • New challenges added regularly by our maker community
Go to CTF Platform
Academy

Structured Learning Paths

The Academy provides guided, module-based courses that walk you through cybersecurity fundamentals all the way to advanced topics. Think of it as a structured curriculum designed by practitioners — not a generic MOOC.

  • Beginner to advanced tracks across every major domain
  • Interactive labs paired with every theory module
  • Progress tracking and milestone certificates
  • Written by and reviewed by active security professionals
  • Integrates with the CTF platform — labs reinforce challenge skills
  • Self-paced — learn on your schedule
Explore Academy
Private Mentorship

1-on-1 Expert Guidance

The Mentorship Guide program connects you directly with experienced members of the CYLSEC community for personalised, focused sessions tailored to your goals — whether that's preparing for certifications, landing a job, or mastering a niche area.

  • Matched with a mentor based on your goals and skill level
  • Regular 1-on-1 sessions (schedule set between you and your mentor)
  • Custom roadmap built around your specific targets
  • Career guidance: CVs, interview prep, certification strategy
  • Access to mentor's curated resources and notes
  • Ongoing accountability and progress check-ins
